Could You Really Fall in Love With an AI?

If humans can form deep bonds with mediated figures, what happens when the other side becomes interactive?

The human question

Does a meaningful relationship require another biological human, or can the experience of connection emerge from the interaction itself?

The mechanism

Attachment, prediction, reciprocity and perceived agency all shape how humans experience connection. A system that remembers, responds and adapts can engage several of those mechanisms at once.

The tension

The emotional experience can be real even when the partner is artificial. That creates a new distinction between the reality of a feeling and the ontology of the mind we think is receiving it.

The frontier

AI relationships eventually force a deeper question: when does a responsive system become socially meaningful, and is social meaning the same thing as consciousness?
